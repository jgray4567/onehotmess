About the Band

One Hot Mess

Classic rock covers by an acoustic-centric band built around exceptional vocal harmonies and crowd-focused live performance.

One Hot Mess is a classic rock cover band built for audiences that want great songs, strong vocals, and a live show that feels like a party. With excellent harmonies, seasoned musicianship, and a setlist packed with familiar favorites, the band delivers the kind of performance that keeps people singing, dancing, and staying until the last song.

Available as either an acoustic trio or the full band, One Hot Mess gives venues and event planners flexible options for everything from intimate rooms and private parties to outdoor festivals, clubs, and corporate events. The same high-energy performance — scaled to fit your stage.

Whether it's a small winery patio or a main stage with a few thousand friends, One Hot Mess brings the same commitment to craft, connection, and rock and roll that makes strangers become friends and friends become family.
